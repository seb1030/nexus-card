Nexus Card ("Nexus," "we," "us") is a digital business card and relationship manager. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your card is public because you share it; everything else is private to you; we don't sell personal data; and viewer tracking is deliberately coarse and disclosed.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Your card: name, title, company, photo or logo, phone, email, and links you choose to display. You control which fields appear via per-field toggles.
  • Your contacts: people you save — their card details, information they share back through your card's share-back form, or details you capture by scanning a paper business card.
  • Your private notes, reminders, tags, and pipeline stages. These are visible only to you (and, on Team plans, to workspace admins where the workspace owns the contact).
  • Account email, if you secure your account or subscribe to a paid plan.

Information collected automatically

  • Card view events: when someone opens your card link, we record the view, the link they clicked, and a timestamp. We do not collect any location data from viewers — not precise, not approximate, not derived from IP address. Every card page displays a notice that views are shared with the card owner.
  • "Where we met" auto-tagging (opt-in only): if you enable it, we use your device location and calendar at the moment of an exchange to suggest an event tag. This is off by default and only ever applies to your own account.
  • Usage and device data: standard logs (app version, device type, crash reports) used to operate and improve the service.

On-device processing

Paper-card scanning (OCR) runs on your device. Card images and recognized text are not sent to our servers unless you save the resulting contact.

2. How we use information

  • To operate your card, contacts, reminders, analytics, and sync.
  • To notify you of engagement (e.g., "your card was viewed") if you're on a plan that includes it.
  • To bill paid subscriptions and prevent abuse.
  • To improve the product using aggregated, de-identified usage patterns.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your contacts' information for advertising or to build cross-customer profiles.

3. Sharing

  • What you share on purpose: your card is visible to anyone with its link or QR. Information a contact submits through share-back goes to the card owner they submitted it to.
  • Service providers: hosting, payments (e.g., Stripe), and email delivery vendors, bound by data-processing agreements. See the Legal hub for the current subprocessor list.
  • Team workspaces: on Team plans, workspace admins can see contacts and pipeline data captured through workspace-owned cards. Your personal notes on personal contacts remain yours.
  • Legal: if required by law, or to protect users from fraud or abuse.

4. Your contacts' rights

People you meet have rights too. If someone asks us to remove information about them held in a Nexus user's contacts, we will honor verified deletion requests as required by applicable law and notify the account holder. Share-back is always optional and clearly labeled; recipients of your card never need an account.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your data is retained while your account is active.
  • You can delete individual contacts and notes at any time. You can delete your entire account from the My Card tab — this is immediate and permanent, and cascades to your card, links, contacts, reminders, history and analytics. Encrypted backups are purged on their normal rotation, within 30 days.
  • If you cancel a paid plan, your data stays intact on the free tier — features are limited, data is not.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: the My Card tab has an Export button that downloads everything we hold about you as machine-readable JSON — profile, card, links, contacts, reminders, history and analytics.
  • Correction: edit your card and contacts directly in the app.
  • Deletion: the My Card tab has a Delete button that permanently removes your account. Or email privacy@nexuscard.app and we will respond within 30 days.
  • GDPR (EEA/UK): our lawful bases are contract performance (running your card and CRM), legitimate interest (security, product analytics), and consent (marketing email). You may object or withdraw consent at any time and lodge complaints with your supervisory authority.
  • CCPA/CPRA (California): we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CPRA. You may exercise access and deletion rights without discrimination.
  • Viewer tracking: card owners cannot turn off the viewer disclosure. If you don't want a card owner notified of your view, don't open the card.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Account access uses passwordless magic links or passkeys — we never store passwords. Offline contact data cached on your device is protected by your device's security.

8. Children

Nexus is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. International transfers

We process data in the United States. Where required, transfers from the EEA/UK rely on Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Changes

We'll notify you of material changes by email or in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect.
