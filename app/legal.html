Acceptable Use Policy

Nexus exists to help real people keep real relationships. You may not use it to:

  • Impersonate another person or organization, or publish a card with false identity information.
  • Send unsolicited bulk outreach (spam) to contacts, or use captured contact data in violation of anti-spam laws (CAN-SPAM, CASL, GDPR/PECR).
  • Scrape, harvest, or enrich data about individuals who never exchanged information with you.
  • Upload malware, attempt unauthorized access, probe or overload the service.
  • Resell or white-label the service without a written agreement.

Violations can lead to suspension or termination under §10 of the Terms.

Subprocessors

Production service providers we expect to use (final list to be confirmed at launch; customers on Team plans will be notified 30 days before changes):

  • Supabase — database, auth, and realtime infrastructure (US region).
  • Vercel — web hosting and edge delivery for public card pages.
  • Stripe — payment processing; we never see or store card numbers.
  • Resend (or similar) — transactional email: magic links, reminder digests.
  • Apple APNs / Google FCM — push notification delivery.
